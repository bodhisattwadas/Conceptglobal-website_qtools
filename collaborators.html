Our Collaborators & Technology Partners

Quantum Tools and Services collaborates with international instrumentation innovators and pioneering hardware ventures. Through these partnerships, we ensure that Indian researchers, national laboratories, and industry leaders receive seamless access to world-class tools with domestic technical support and warranty backing.

DACTools LLC

USA

DACTools is a recognized leader in custom diamond anvil cells, resistively heated cells, in-situ Raman cells, and precision ruby fluorescence calibrants. QTS serves as the exclusive distributor in India, delivering direct product consultation, gasket EDM drilling, and hands-on experimental setup training.

Razorbill Instruments

UK

Based in the United Kingdom, Razorbill Instruments designs and manufactures industry-standard cryogenic uniaxial strain and stress cells with integrated capacitive sensors. As exclusive India partners, QTS provides turnkey cryostat mounting pucks, low-noise cryo wiring, and on-site user support.

TraceXero

CLEANTECH

TraceXero pioneers scalable direct carbon capture (DAC) solutions for atmospheric greenhouse gas mitigation. QTS acts as product development consultant and precision fabrication partner, optimizing mechanical contactors, flow chambers, and cleanroom sensor integration.
