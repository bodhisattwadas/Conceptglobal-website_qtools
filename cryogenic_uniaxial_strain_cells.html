Quantum Tuning Under Controlled Deformation

Uniaxial strain provides a clean, symmetry-breaking thermodynamic tuning parameter that alters electronic band structures, lifts orbital degeneracies, and probes unconventional quantum states.

Performance Parameter Specification Range Operational Context
Operating Temperature Below 300 mK up to 350 K Dilution refrigerators, helium flow cryostats, closed-cycle systems
Magnetic Field Compatibility Up to 20+ Tesla Titanium Grade 5 and BeCu non-magnetic construction
Actuation Mechanism Piezoelectric stack actuators Reversible, continuous bidirectional strain (tension & compression)

TRANSPORT & RESISTIVITY

Elastoresistivity & Magnetoresistance

Study nematic fluctuations, Lifshitz transitions, and anisotropic scattering in iron-based superconductors, cuprates, and heavy-fermion materials under controlled uniaxial deformation.

SPECTROSCOPY & OPTICS

Raman, PL & Synchrotron In-Situ Optics

Optical access options permit in-situ micro-Raman scattering, photoluminescence, and synchrotron X-ray diffraction while dynamically cycling strain across phase boundaries.

CUSTOM ADAPTERS

Cryostat Mounting & Thermal Anchoring

QTS designs and fabricates custom OFHC copper thermal brackets, sapphire heat sinks, and cryostat spacer pucks to guarantee thermal equilibrium at millikelvin baselines.
