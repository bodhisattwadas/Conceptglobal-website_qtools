Specialty Metallurgy & Machining Operations

Unlike commercial machine shops, QTS specializes strictly in materials used in cryogenic, superconducting, high-magnetic-field, and ultra-high-vacuum experiments.

MICRO-EDM DRILLING

Micro-Hole Drilling in Hardened Alloys

Precision wire and sinker EDM capabilities enable drilling apertures as small as 20 µm through hardened rhenium, tungsten carbide, and BeCu gaskets without burrs or residual thermal stress.

CLEANROOM PREPARATION

Ultrasonic Degreasing & Clean Packaging

All finished components undergo multi-stage ultrasonic chemical degreasing (citric acid passivation, solvent rinses, deionized water baths) and are sealed in clean packaging ready for direct insertion into UHV chambers.
